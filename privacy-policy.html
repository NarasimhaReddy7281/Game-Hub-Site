01Introduction

Welcome to Game Hub, a mobile gaming platform featuring multiple mini games, available on Android. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use our app, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information. By using Game Hub, you agree to the terms described in this policy.

02Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.

The following non-personal data may be collected automatically, primarily through third-party advertising and analytics services:

  • Device type and operating system version
  • App usage and interaction data (e.g., which games are played)
  • Advertising identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID)
  • Approximate location (country/region level, for ad targeting only)
  • Crash reports and performance data

This data is collected in aggregate and cannot be used to identify you personally.

03How we use information

Any data collected is used solely for the following purposes:

  • Displaying relevant advertisements within the app
  • Improving app performance and fixing bugs
  • Understanding general usage patterns to improve user experience
  • Complying with legal obligations

We do not sell, trade, or rent any user data to third parties.

04Permissions used

Game Hub may request the following permissions on your Android device:

  • Internet Access (INTERNET) — required to load advertisements and enable online features within the app.
  • Network State (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) — used to check connectivity before loading ads or online content.

No permissions are used to access your camera, microphone, contacts, storage, or location beyond what is listed above.

05Third-party services

Game Hub may use the following third-party services. Each service operates under its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob — used to display advertisements within the app. AdMob may collect device information and advertising identifiers to serve personalized ads. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Services — used for app distribution and optional analytics. Google Privacy Policy

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these third-party providers for more detail.

06Data security

We take reasonable precautions to protect the limited data associated with your use of Game Hub. Since we do not collect or store personal information on our own servers, the risk to your personal data is minimal. Third-party services used within the app maintain their own security standards in accordance with industry best practices.

07Children's privacy

Game Hub is designed for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us at ugra7281@gmail.com and we will take prompt action to address the concern.

Advertisements served through Google AdMob comply with applicable regulations regarding advertising to children.

08Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of Game Hub after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

09Contact us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at ugra7281@gmail.com.
